# RiftCity Crime Minigames V5.2

## Pickpocketing
- Replaced the live approach/opening-meter system with Target Chain + Pocket Zone.
- Three pedestrians appear at once and rotate automatically every 18 seconds.
- No Let Pass / Abort button.
- Selecting a target is free; Nerve is only spent when GRAB resolves.
- Smooth CSS-driven needle sweeps across a compact pocket track.
- Center pocket zone gives the strongest success/reward modifier.
- Edge hits are weaker; outer danger zones sharply increase Heat/arrest pressure.
- Harder targets make the needle move faster.
- Guard Crackdown directly penalizes the minigame attempt.
- Mastery still reveals wealth, awareness, exact odds, and danger intel.

## Data Theft
- Added an abstract Memory Trace minigame.
- Player memorizes 3–5 symbols (difficulty-scaled), then repeats the sequence.
- Correct sequence grants success/reward bonuses and slightly cleaner Heat.
- Incorrect sequence penalizes the attempt.
- No real hacking procedures or commands are modeled.

## Safecracking
- Added a fictional Precision Dial minigame.
- A marker sweeps smoothly across a dial track.
- Locking near the center sync zone grants the strongest attempt bonus.
- Near-center hits grant a smaller bonus; poor timing penalizes the attempt.
- No real safe-opening methods are modeled.

## Existing unique crime loops retained
- Scavenging: real-time district opportunities.
- Shoplifting: live store conditions + basket greed/suspicion.
- Graffiti: reputation/location progression.
- Target crimes: rotating/scoutable targets.
- Passive operations: timers and risk-build cashout.
- Major crimes: branching event decisions.
