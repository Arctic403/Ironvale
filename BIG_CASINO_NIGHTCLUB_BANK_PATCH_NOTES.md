# RiftCity Big Casino / Nightclub / Bank Patch

## Casino
- Keeps RiftCity cash wagering, no casino play cooldown, and 75 bonus-chip cap.
- Adds Roulette, Baccarat, Craps, and Casino War alongside Blackjack and full Hold'em.
- Expands slots to six themed machines with 5- and 6-reel layouts.
- Adds Gold Rush Mine with a six-reel mining theme.
- Adds a persistent casino jackpot pool funded by a small portion of slot wagers.
- Adds rare random jackpot triggers and multi-symbol payouts.
- Keeps animated Rift Wheel and timed Rift Downs horse races.

## Bank
- Existing `bank` balance becomes the main checking account for save compatibility.
- Adds savings account, transfers, transaction ledger, balance history, and SVG balance graph.
- Checking and savings earn different daily interest rates.
- Adds four time/deposit-gated investment tiers with caps, terms, and fixed return rates.
- Active investments persist in saves and automatically mature back into checking through the normal game tick.
- Adds lifetime-deposit and account-age progression for investment unlocks.

## Nightclub
- Adds Pulse Nightclub as a real Entertainment District map location and screen.
- Adds dance floor, guest DJ, social lounge, nightclub reputation, visits, rank progression, and VIP unlock.
- Replaces the old future Nightlife Strip marker.

## Save migration
- New finance, jackpot, and nightclub fields receive safe defaults when older saves load.
