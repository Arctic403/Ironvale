# RiftCity World Bible — V1 Foundation

## Core identity

RiftCity is a persistent browser life/crime simulation set in an original modern world.
The player's starting point is **RiftCity**, their permanent home city.

The city is not a movement-based RPG map. The city screen is a home hub for local services and activities.

## The bigger world

Beyond RiftCity are other cities and regions. Travel will eventually use destination-based, real-time journeys.
Each destination should have its own economy, organizations, opportunities, risks, and culture.

Travel is optional: players should travel because a destination offers something meaningful.

## The Rift

The Rift is the central long-term mystery of the setting. It affected technology, trade, politics, and the balance of power.
V1 should hint at it without explaining everything.

The story should unfold in layers:

1. **Life** — jobs, crime, training, education, property, money.
2. **City** — corporations, government, gangs, factions, markets.
3. **World** — travel, international organizations, different economies and cultures.
4. **Rift** — the deeper mystery behind the world's unusual history.

## Design rules

- Mechanics can be genre-inspired; RiftCity's expression must be original.
- Do not copy another game's names, characters, artwork, dialogue, UI, lore, or code.
- Every major mechanic should have an in-world explanation.
- Single-player progression is the foundation; multiplayer can layer onto it later.
- New destinations should be data-driven so the world can expand without rewriting the core engine.
