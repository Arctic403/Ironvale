# Crime Careers + Graffiti Overhaul

## What changed
- Reworked the Crimes page into four distinct loops: Targets, Operations, Graffiti, and Major Jobs.
- Pickpocket, Burglary, and Vehicle Theft now use rotating target boards with optional scouting.
- Retail Theft, Package Swipe, and Cargo Theft are fast single-resolution crimes instead of repeating 3-choice flows.
- Major Robbery, Warehouse Job, Bank Job, and Major Heist keep the branching situation system.
- Added separate crime-family progression: Street Theft, Burglary, Vehicle Crime, Fraud, Street Art, and Organized Crime.
- Added Street Reputation and reputation ranks.
- Added graffiti spots that unlock through Street Reputation, grant Street Art skill, add Heat, and can add small faction visibility.
- Added passive/semi-passive crime operations with setup costs, timers, payout ranges, detection risk, and skill scaling.
- Added abstract Card Skimming and Email Fraud operations plus later organized/vehicle operations. These are deliberately game abstractions and do not model real-world methods.
- Existing one-use crime tools can now also be used on target-board crimes.
- Running/ready crime operations now appear in the global Effects strip.
- Updated the in-game Wiki with the new crime loops, skill families, operations, and graffiti system.
- Added migration-safe save defaults for all new crime progression/state fields.

## Beta tuning
- Target boards rotate every 5 minutes.
- Passive operation timers are intentionally short for beta testing.
- Up to 3 passive crime operations can run at once during beta.
